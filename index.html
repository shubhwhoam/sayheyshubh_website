Frequently Asked Questions

What is SayHeyShubh?

SayHeyShubh is a completely student-driven peer-to-peer education repository designed explicitly for Delhi University students to gain premium access to undergrad science notes and community systems.

Is SayHeyShubh officially affiliated with Delhi University?

No. SayHeyShubh is a completely independent student project created by Shubham Kumar. It holds no official administrative affiliations with the university administration.

Which semesters are currently optimized live?

BSc Zoology, Microbiology, Botany and Life Science streams are fully live across core foundational semesters with DU PYQs.

Are the practical files and lab manuals verified?

Yes. All practical files and spotting manuals are cross-checked by top-performing students who have already passed those specific internal and external DU exams.

We actively update our database after every DU semester examination cycle, ensuring you always have the most recent exam trends and question formats.
