📋 DU Semester 4 Syllabus Overview

Official DU Syllabus

The fourth semester of BSc Zoology (Hons.) introduces students to the evolutionary history of vertebrates, the complex stages of embryonic development, and the fascinating world of animal behaviour. The core subjects include:

🦎 (DSC-10) Comparative Anatomy of Vertebrates: Includes Integumentary, Digestive, Circulatory, Respiratory, Skeletal, Nervous, Sense Organs, and Urinogenital Systems.
🥚 (DSC-11) Developmental Biology: Covers early to late embryonic development, and implications of developmental biology.
🐒 (DSC-12) Animal Behaviour: Focuses on Mechanisms, Patterns, Communication, Social Behaviour, Altruism, and Sexual Behaviour.

(DSC-10) Comparative Anatomy of Vertebrates

Unit 1: Integumentary System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Structure and derivatives of integument.

Unit 2: Digestive System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Alimentary Canal and Associated Glands (comparative alimentary canal, stomach modifications, ruminant stomach) Dentition (tooth attachment, types, succession, cusp patterns, mammalian dentition, carnassial teeth, dental formula, baleen).

Unit 3: Circulatory System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: General plan of circulation; Evolution of heart and aortic arches.

Unit 4: Respiratory System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Skin, gills, lungs, accessory respiratory organs in fishes, air sacs. Mechanism of respiration in fishes, amphibians and birds or aves.

Unit 5: Skeletal System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Outline of axial and appendicular skeleton; Concept of neurocranium, dermatocranium and splanchnocranium; Structure of a typical vertebra and its classification based on centrum; Jaw suspensorium; General plan of girdles and limbs.

Unit 6: Nervous System

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Comparative account of brain of vertebrates; Cranial nerves in mammals.

(DSC-11) Developmental Biology

Unit 1: Introduction

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Historical perspectives and basic concepts: Phases of development, Pattern formation, Differentiation and growth, Cytoplasmic determinants.

Unit 2: Early Embryonic Development

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Gametogenesis: oogenesis, spermatogenesis; Types of eggs, Egg membranes; Fertilization (External and Internal), Blocks to polyspermy; Planes and patterns of cleavage; Types of Blastula; Fate maps; Gastrulation in frog and chick, Embryonic induction and organizers.

Unit 3: Late Embryonic Development

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Fate of Germ Layers; Extra-embryonic membranes in birds; Implantation of embryo in humans, structure, types, and functions of placenta.

Unit 4: Post Embryonic Development

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Metamorphosis and its hormonal regulation in amphibians and insects; Regeneration: Modes of regeneration, epimorphosis (Salamander limb regenration), morphallaxis (Regeneration in Hydra) and compensatory regeneration (Liver regeneration); Ageing: concepts and theories.

Unit 5: Implications of Developmental Biology

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Teratogenesis: Teratogenic agents and their effects on embryonic development; in-vitro fertilization, Embryonic stem cell (ESC), Amniocentesis.

(DSC-12) Animal Behaviour

Unit 1: Introduction to Animal Behaviour

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Origin and history of ethology; Pioneers of modern ethology: Karl von Frisch, Ivan Pavlov, Konrad Lorenz, Niko Tinbergen; Proximate and ultimate causes of behavior.

Unit 2: Mechanisms of Behaviour

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Innate behaviour, Instinct, Stimulus filtering, Sign stimuli, Code breakers.

Unit 3: Patterns of Behaviour

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Orientation: Primary and secondary orientation; Kinesis - orthokinesis, klinokinesis; Taxis: tropotaxis and klinotaxis, menotaxis (light compass orientation). Learning: Associative learning, Classical and operant conditioning, Habituation, Imprinting; Reasoning: Intelligence and artificial intelligence.

Unit 4: Communication

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Importance of communication; Role of Tactile, Chemical, Auditory, Visual stimuli in communication.

Unit 5: Social Behaviour

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Innate behaviour, Instinct, Stimulus filtering, Sign stimuli, Code breakers.

Unit 6: Altruism

Important Qs

Topics Covered: Altruism, Inclusive fitness, Kin Selection, Hamilton’s rule
