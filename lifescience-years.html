1st Year Foundations

Looking for bsc life science 1st semester notes? The bsc life science syllabus du follows (UGCF 2022) has three DSC papers in Semester 1: Plant Diversity and Systematics (BOT-DSC-1), Basic Concepts of Organic Chemistry (CHEM-DSC-01) and Diversity of Animals (ZOO-DSC-01). Each one is split into units here, so you can open exactly the part you need.
