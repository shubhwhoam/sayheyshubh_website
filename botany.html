The Complete DU Botany Syllabus Roadmap

Our structured notes break down the vast Delhi University Botany (Hons) NEP/FYUP curriculum into focused, high-yield PDFs.

1st Year Foundations

The first year builds your base with Microbial Diversity (viruses, bacteria, and their economic importance) and Phycology & Aquatic Biodiversity in Semester 1, followed by Mycology, Phytopathology and Archegoniates (Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms) in Semester 2.

2nd Year Core Papers

The second year shifts to Plant Anatomy, Plant Systematics, Cell Biology, and Plant Physiology & Metabolism, which are the highest-weightage and most diagram-heavy papers of the entire degree.

3rd Year Advanced Applications

Final year studies turn applied, covering Plant Ecology, Genetics & Plant Breeding, Economic Botany & Biotechnology, and elective papers, designed to help you translate theory into strong exam answers.
